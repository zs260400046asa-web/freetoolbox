02

Good to know

Private by design

Most background removers upload your photo to their servers. This one doesn't — the AI model is built into the page itself, and every photo is processed on your own device.

That means no queues, no per-image credits, and your pictures stay yours. Close the tab and nothing remains.

Tips for a cleaner cutout

  • Use a photo where the subject stands out clearly from what's behind it.
  • Good, even lighting beats a dark or blurry shot every time.
  • Fine hair and fur work best against a plain, contrasting background.
  • For marketplace listings, export on white — it's what most platforms expect.
03

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes — unlimited images, full resolution, no watermark and no account. Because the processing happens on your device instead of an expensive server, there's no per-image cost to pass on to you.

Why did the first image take longer?

The first run starts the AI engine in your browser, which takes a few seconds. The model itself is built into the page — nothing needs to download first. A larger full-quality model can download in the background afterwards; when it is ready, you can re-run the same photo with it for finer edges.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. The photo never leaves your device. The AI model is part of this page — the only thing that may download afterwards is an optional larger model, never your photo.

What image size can I use?

JPG, PNG and WebP all work. Very large photos (over 4096 px on the longest side) are scaled down for processing so your browser doesn't run out of memory — still plenty for listings, social posts and print at reasonable sizes.

The edges aren't perfect. What can I do?

Every cutout is cleaned up automatically — background fragments the AI mistook for the subject are removed and the outline is tightened. If edges still aren't perfect, try a sharper photo with better contrast between the subject and the background, or re-run with the full-quality model when it has downloaded. Busy backgrounds and motion blur are the two things AI cutouts struggle with most.

Background removal runs on ONNX Runtime Web — entirely in your browser. The built-in U²-Net model and the runtime ship with this page, so processing never depends on a download; the larger open-source RMBG-1.4 model by BRIA AI is fetched in the background when your connection allows, and offered as an optional full-quality re-run.
